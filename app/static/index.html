🏔️ DEM (Elevation Model) Terrain Grid
Represents the Earth's surface as a pixel grid, with each pixel containing an elevation value. Essential for understanding real-world terrain and modeling natural relief.
Used for: Hydrology, engineering, construction, mining, agriculture, telecommunications, environmental monitoring, disaster response.
⬇️ View PNG 💾 GeoTIFF (32-bit)
🏙️ DSM (Surface Model) Top Surface
Captures all visible surface features, including the top reflective surface (tree canopies, rooftops, power lines, vehicles) plus ground in 3D relief.
Used for: Modeling landscapes with fine details, urban planning, line-of-sight analysis, and environmental conservation.
⬇️ View PNG 💾 GeoTIFF (32-bit)
📐 DTM (Terrain Model) Bare Earth
Represents the bare earth surface with structural vectors (breaklines, ridges, valleys, and topographic contours) stripped of vegetation and buildings.
Used for: Engineering design, infrastructure routing, contour mapping, cut/fill calculation, and advanced cartography.
